Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 Sticks(8oz) Unsalted Butter, softened
  • 1/4 C. (2oz) White Sugar
  • 1/4 C. (2oz) Turbinado Sugar
  • 1 3/4 C. (12oz) Light Brown Sugar, Packed
  • 2 Eggs
  • 2 Teaspoons (1/4oz) Vanilla Extract
  • 3 1/2 C. (1lb) All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon Baking Soda
  • 3/4 Teaspoon Salt
  • 1 lb Chocolate, chopped into 1/2in. Chunks (best quality available)

Steps

  1. Cream the butter and the sgars until light and fluffy. Scrape doen the sides of the bowl. Continue mixing whole adding the eggs one at a time. Make sure each egg is incorporated before adding the next. Add the Vanilla. Scrape down the bowel with a spatula. Combine the flour, soda, and salt in another bowl.With a whisk, stir to combine. With the machine on low, slowly add the flour. Mix until just combined, taking care not to over mix. With a spatula fold in the chocolate.
  2. If you choose, and I reccommend that you do, sprinkle a very fine dusting of good quality sea salt. I use Maldon sea salt.
  3. Bake at 360 degree F for 12 minutes. They should be lightly golden on the outside but still look gooey inside.
